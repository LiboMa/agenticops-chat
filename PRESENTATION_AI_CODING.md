# PPT: The New Calculus of AI-based Coding
## Paradigm Shift in Software Engineering (2025-2026)

---

## Slide 1: Title
**Title:** The New Calculus of AI-based Coding
**Subtitle:** Navigating the Shift from Writing to Verifying
**Author:** Analysis based on Joe Magerramov, Matt Welsh, and Gergely Orosz

---

## Slide 2: The Economic Shift (The "Calculus")
*   **The Old World:** Writing code was the primary cost. Refactoring and testing were "expensive" extras.
*   **The New World:** Generating code is nearly free (marginal cost → 0).
*   **The New Bottleneck:** The human capacity to **Review, Verify, and Trust** the output.
*   **Core Idea:** Software engineering is no longer limited by typing speed, but by **Judgment Speed**.

---

## Slide 3: Comprehension Debt
*   **Definition:** The gap between the volume of code generated and the volume of code understood by the team.
*   **The Risk:** Systems become "black boxes" where no single human knows how the logic works.
*   **The Solution:** Shifting from "Line-by-Line Review" to "Constraint-Based Validation" (Automated tests, property-based testing).

---

## Slide 4: Role Transition: The Orchestrator
*   **From "Code Writer" to "System Conductor":**
    *   **Old Role:** Implementing algorithms and managing syntax.
    *   **New Role:** Defining high-level intent, setting guardrails, and auditing AI "hallucinations."
*   **Key Skill:** The ability to "Debug at the Architectural Level" rather than the "Syntax Level."

---

## Slide 5: Expert View: The End of Programming (Matt Welsh)
*   **The Theory:** Programming languages (Python, Java) are becoming "low-level assembly."
*   **The Future:** Natural language and "Intent-Based" declarations are the new source code.
*   **Impact:** CS education must pivot from "how to write" to "how to architect and verify."

---

## Slide 6: The Velocity Gap (Gergely Orosz)
*   **The Friction:** Developers can now build features 5x faster, but:
    *   Security reviews are still slow.
    *   Legal/Compliance checks are still slow.
    *   Product-Market fit doesn't happen 5x faster.
*   **The Trap:** High velocity without high-quality verification leads to "faster failure."

---

## Slide 7: Engineering Strategy for 2026
*   **Invest in "Defensive Automation":** If AI writes the code, humans (or other AIs) must write the "Turing Tests" for that code.
*   **Observability First:** Treat AI-generated code as "untrusted third-party code" — monitor its behavior in real-time.
*   **Iterative Refinement:** Use AI to explore 10 different architectures before committing to one.

---

## Slide 8: Conclusion
*   **Summary:** AI has commoditized the "how." The "what" and "why" are now more valuable than ever.
*   **The Winner:** The engineer who masters the **Feedback Loop** between Human Intent and AI Execution.
*   **Closing:** "The code doesn't matter; the system behavior does."
