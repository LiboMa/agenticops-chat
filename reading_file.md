A man goes to the doctor and says, "Doctor, wherever I touch my body, it hurts."

The doctor looks confused and asks, "What do you mean?"

The man says, "When I touch my shoulder, it hurts! If I touch my knee, Ouch! When I touch my forehead, it hurts! What's wrong with me?"

The doctor examines him for a moment, sighs, and says, "I know exactly what's wrong with you. Your finger is broken."
